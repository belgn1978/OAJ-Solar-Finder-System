# P1 print and test guide

## Files to send to the print service

Send all four STL files in the `stl` folder:

1. `OAJ_SFS_P1_body.stl`
2. `OAJ_SFS_P1_nacatin_adapter.stl`
3. `OAJ_SFS_P1_pinhole_plate.stl`
4. `OAJ_SFS_P1_target_plate.stl`

All dimensions are in millimetres. Request one of each part.

## Recommended print specification

| Item | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Process | FDM/FFF |
| Material | PETG preferred; ASA is also suitable |
| Layer height | 0.20 mm |
| Walls/perimeters | 4 |
| Top and bottom layers | 5 |
| Infill | 30% gyroid or cubic |
| Supports | None intended |
| Body orientation | Flat base on build plate |
| Adapter orientation | Telescope-foot base on build plate |
| Plate orientation | Broad face flat on build plate |

Print the body and pinhole plate in a dark, opaque colour. Print the target plate in white, cream, pale grey, or another light colour. A dark permanent marker or model paint can be applied to the recessed crosshair after printing.

PETG is preferred over ordinary PLA because the finder may be exposed to direct sunlight and elevated temperatures. Do not leave the device unattended in strong sunlight or in a hot vehicle.

## Critical dimensions for the print quotation

- Largest part: 90 × 30 × 10 mm
- Nacatin adapter: 39.3 × 29.5 × 13 mm
- Each plate: 41 × 34 × 3 mm in its supplied print orientation
- Universal sliding fit: 0.25 mm clearance per side
- Plate-slot fit: 0.25 mm clearance per side
- Pinhole pilot: 0.8 mm nominal

## Assembly

1. Check the Nacatin adapter in the empty finder shoe before attaching the universal body. It should insert without heavy force and should not rock excessively.
2. Slide the adapter tongue into the open rear of the body's underside socket until the positive stop reaches the front of the socket.
3. Insert the pinhole plate in the front slot with the aperture at the top.
4. Insert the target plate into the 70 mm position first. The recessed crosshair must face the pinhole plate.
5. If either printed joint is tight, lightly deburr the edges. Do not force a part into the telescope shoe.

## Pinhole finishing

FDM printers may partially close a 0.8 mm hole. Check the aperture against a bright background before assembly. If necessary, turn a 0.8 mm drill bit through the pilot **by hand**. Remove loose plastic and confirm that the opening is round and unobstructed. Do not use a powered drill while holding the plate.

## First mechanical test

| Test | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Telescope foot | Slides in, seats fully, and is retained by the shoe screw/notch |
| Foot stability | No unsafe rocking or lifting |
| Universal dovetail | Slides fully to its stop without cracking or excessive play |
| Plate slots | Both plates seat vertically and remain in place when gently moved |
| Alignment | Pinhole and target centre marks appear level by eye |

The Nacatin foot height is currently estimated at 5.5 mm. Stop and revise the adapter if the telescope shoe does not retain it correctly.

## First sunlight test

1. Fit and inspect a certified full-aperture solar filter on the telescope before taking it outside.
2. Cover or remove every other optical finder.
3. Attach the solar finder and start with the target at 70 mm.
4. Without looking through any aperture, move the telescope while watching only the rear target.
5. Centre the projected spot on the target crosshair.
6. Verify the Sun is centred using the filtered main telescope at low magnification.
7. Test the remaining target positions and record which gives the clearest, easiest-to-centre spot.

Never use the projection finder as a substitute for the telescope's objective filter.

## Feedback needed for P2

- Actual Nacatin shoe fit and any required width/height correction
- Universal dovetail tightness
- Plate-slot tightness
- Best target distance
- Visibility and size of the projected spot
- Any softening, warping, or discolouration after the sunlight test
