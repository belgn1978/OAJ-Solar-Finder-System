# Main Body

## Purpose

The main body is the permanent part of the system.

It accepts interchangeable adapters underneath and removable target plates above.

---

## P1 contains

- Adapter socket
- Six target positions at 45, 50, 55, 60, 65, and 70 mm
- Front plate mount
- Rear plate slots
- Weight-reduction pockets

---

## Design Goals

- Printable without supports
- Lightweight
- Strong
- Easy assembly
- Modular

---

## Status

P1 STL generated and mesh validated.

## Future features

- Folding plates
- Adjustable pinhole
- Optional locking clip
- Colour inserts
